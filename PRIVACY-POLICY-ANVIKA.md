# Privacy Policy for Anvika

**Effective Date:** June 17, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Anvika** ("Anvika", "we", "our", or "us"), developed and operated by **AshishTheDev**.

Anvika is a menstrual and wellness tracking application designed to help users monitor menstrual cycles, symptoms, moods, and related health information.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

By using Anvika, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information including:

* Name
* Email address
* Menstrual cycle dates
* Flow intensity
* Symptoms
* Mood information
* Notes and observations
* Other wellness-related information entered by you

### 2.2 Google Sign-In Information

If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we may access and locally store:

* Your name
* Your email address

Authentication is provided through Firebase Authentication.

### 2.3 Analytics and Diagnostic Information

Anvika uses certain Google Firebase services including:

* Firebase Authentication
* Firebase Analytics
* Firebase Crashlytics

These services may automatically collect limited technical information such as:

* Device type
* Operating system version
* App version
* Crash reports
* Anonymous usage events
* Diagnostic information

This information helps improve app stability, reliability, and performance.

---

## 3. How Your Health Data Is Stored

Menstrual cycle records, symptoms, moods, wellness logs, and personal notes are stored **only on your device using local storage mechanisms**.

We do not upload, synchronize, or maintain copies of your menstrual health logs on our servers.

At this time, Anvika does not provide cloud synchronization or remote backup services.

Users may choose to manually export their data.

---

## 4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

* Personalize your experience
* Display cycle history
* Generate menstrual cycle predictions
* Improve application performance
* Diagnose crashes and software issues
* Maintain authentication sessions
* Provide wellness insights

Cycle predictions available in the current version of Anvika are generated locally using information stored on your device.

Future versions may introduce optional AI-powered features that communicate with third-party services. If such functionality becomes available, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.

---

## 5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not rent your personal information.

We do not share menstrual logs, symptoms, moods, notes, or cycle history with advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be processed by trusted service providers including Google Firebase solely to provide authentication, analytics, and crash reporting services.

---

## 6. Data Retention

Health logs remain stored on your device until:

* You delete specific entries
* You uninstall the application
* You clear application storage
* You choose to export and remove your information

Crash reports and analytics information may be retained by Google Firebase according to Google's applicable retention policies.

---

## 7. Security

We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information processed by Anvika.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are encouraged to maintain device security measures such as passwords, biometric locks, and updated operating systems.
